1 · Position

Secure the phone on a flat surface, screen facing up. Point its physical top edge toward the ship’s bow. Leave it there.

2 · Start and zero

Start sensors, then tap Zero tilt. The 5-second average becomes the local reference; it does not establish the ship’s true level.

3 · Wait for several cycles

Gentle swells need 60–120 seconds. Don’t walk with the phone during a measurement. Opening menus keeps capture running.
